About the spa

A sanctuary to escape to

Enhance Beauty Therapy Ltd has been operating for over 20 years from the tranquil location of Prebbleton, Christchurch. Our luxurious day spa sits in the heart of the Prebbleton Village.

Our earthy toned spa has peaceful music, balmy temperatures and private, spacious treatment rooms for clients who want a sanctuary to escape to. There's plenty of off-street parking, so arriving is as easy as the rest of your visit.

  • Earthy toned, peaceful spa
  • Balmy, comfortable temperatures
  • Private, spacious treatment rooms
  • Plenty of off-street parking
